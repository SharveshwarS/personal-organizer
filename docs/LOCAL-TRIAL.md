# Your first local trial

Open **Personal Organizer** from the desktop shortcut. The latest preview is **0.4.0-preview.1**. Existing setup and records survive this update. Use the installed app for Windows reminders; the browser preview has separate storage.

## Start with your own preferences

Choose a name or leave it blank, select your currency, and choose Sunday or Monday for the start of your week. Finance setup is optional. If you enter an expected income and savings target, they are plans: record the actual income separately when received. Opening balances are the funds you already have at the beginning of today.

## Try the everyday flow

1. Add a task with a few checklist steps and a reminder a few minutes ahead.
2. Click **+** beside **My Lists** and create a list. Add a habit with a unit such as Times or Glasses. Choose either a daily reminder time or an interval such as every 10 minutes or 2 hours, between your chosen start/end times. Add a note with some Markdown.
3. Close the window with X. It should remain in the tray; check whether the Windows reminder arrives. Click the tray icon to reopen.
4. Record an income and an expense in Finance, then review category spending and balances.
5. Rename a list or category in Settings. Choose quiet hours if useful.
6. Export a backup. Quit from the tray menu and reopen to check your records are still there.

You do not have to complete everything at once. These are suggestions for your trial, not checks that have already passed. The more detailed checklist is [FINAL-TEST-CHECKLIST.md](FINAL-TEST-CHECKLIST.md).

## Send useful feedback

Describe the screen, what you did, what you expected, and what happened. Include a screenshot if helpful, hiding private notes and financial details. For reminders, mention whether the app was open, hidden in the tray, or fully quit, and whether Windows Do Not Disturb was enabled.

Continue using the app locally before choosing additional features, the license, or a public release. There is no automatic updater; keep an exported backup before installing later builds. Further updates should preserve your new trial data unless you explicitly request another reset.


## Try the cozy update

Open Journey to visit your garden and firefly, or Focus to begin a session. Complete a task, reach a habit target, or finish a focus session to earn XP. The bell opens snooze controls; Finance includes recurring bills and a monthly category comparison. Habit editing now includes pause dates and keeps schedule history.
