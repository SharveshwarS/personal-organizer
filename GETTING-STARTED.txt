PERSONAL ORGANIZER — 0.4.0-preview.1 LOCAL TRIAL
By Sharveshwar S — https://github.com/SharveshwarS

Automated checks cover the new features. Remaining native checks are documented in the release notes.
Open the Personal Organizer desktop shortcut. Existing setup and records are retained.

FIRST LAUNCH
Choose an optional name, currency and first day of the week.
Enter monthly planning amounts and opening balances, or skip finance setup.
Existing users retain their records and old planning amounts; edit preferences in Settings.

YOUR WORKSPACE
Customize task lists and expense categories in Settings.
Use + beside My Lists to add a list directly from the sidebar.
Add tasks and checklist steps. Schedule tasks or set habit reminder times.
Choosing a task time enables its reminder automatically. Uncheck it for no alert.
Record actual income, expenses and savings transfers in Finance.
Edit a monthly plan or set defaults for future months.

REMINDERS
X keeps the app running in the tray. Click its icon to reopen.
Right-click the tray icon > Quit Personal Organizer to stop it fully.
Configure quiet hours and optional Windows startup in Settings.
Habits offer preset/custom units and daily or interval Windows reminders.
Choose minutes/hours and a same-day start/end time for interval reminders.
Optionally stop interval reminders after reaching the daily target.
After sleep or quiet hours only the latest eligible interval is considered.
The app cannot notify while fully quit or while the PC is asleep/off.

RECOVERY
Settings shows the data folder and latest automatic backup date.
Export a backup to a separate drive before upgrading or changing computers.
New version-2 backups need the new app; older backups remain importable.
Do not reopen a migrated schema-2 database using version 0.2 or earlier.
